Privacy Policy

No Way But Up · Last updated 28 August 2026

The short version. No Way But Up shows no ads and contains no analytics or tracking of any kind. You can play the entire game without an account and without an internet connection. If you choose to sign in, we store your email address, your nickname and your daily scores — nothing else.

Who is responsible

This game is made by an individual developer, not a company.
Controller: Matyáš Doležal, Pardubice, Czech Republic.
Contact: nowaybutup@email.cz

Playing without an account

You can play the whole game — every mode, every purchase you have made, the full shop — without signing in and without a network connection. In that case no data ever leaves your device. Your record, crystals and unlocked items are kept only in your device's local storage.

What we collect if you sign in

Signing in is optional. It exists so your progress survives losing your phone, and so you can appear on the leaderboard.

DataWhyWho can see it
Email address To identify your account. Sign-in uses a one-time code sent by email — there is no password. Only you and the developer
Nickname Shown next to your score. Public on the leaderboard
Friend code (6 characters) Lets friends add you without exchanging emails. Anyone you give it to
Daily results (metres and date) The daily leaderboard and the reward for finishing in the top three. Public on the leaderboard
Game progress (crystals, purchased items, personal record, friend codes you added) Backup, so nothing is lost when you reinstall or change device. Only you and the developer

We do not collect your name, address, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or advertising IDs, and we do not build any profile of you.

No ads, no analytics, no tracking

The game contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking or profiling technology. Nothing about your behaviour is measured or sold. The only permission the app requests is internet access, used solely for the leaderboard, the progress backup and purchases.

Purchases

Crystals can be bought as an optional in-app purchase. Payments are handled entirely by Apple or Google and processed through RevenueCat, which tells the game whether a purchase succeeded. We never see or store your card details.

Services we use

  • Supabase — database and sign-in (your account, scores and progress backup).
  • Resend — sends the one-time sign-in code to your email address.
  • RevenueCat, together with Apple and Google — in-app purchases.
  • GitHub Pages — hosting of the browser version of the game.

These providers process data on our behalf. Some of them operate servers outside the European Union; where that happens, transfers are covered by the safeguards those providers have in place, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Daily results stay on the leaderboard so past days remain comparable.
  • Account, nickname and progress backup are kept while your account exists.
  • When you ask us to delete your account, everything tied to it — account, nickname, scores, progress backup and reward history — is deleted.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, or restrict or object to how we use it. Write to nowaybutup@email.cz from the address you signed in with and we will handle it. You may also complain to your national data protection authority; in the Czech Republic that is the Office for Personal Data Protection (ÚOOÚ).

You can also simply sign out, or delete the app, which removes everything stored on your device.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us their email address, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Significant changes will be announced in the game.
